If you live around Kalighat and have been looking for **scooty training in Kalighat**, here is the honest picture. Zero Fear has one centre, near Netaji Metro in Paschim Putiary, and from Kalighat it is a few stops down the Blue Line.

If you live in Hazra, Bhowanipore or Chetla, you've probably looked at your own para and thought there's no way to learn here, it's too crowded. Fair. You don't learn your first wobble at Hazra crossing. You learn it somewhere quiet, and then you come back and ride the roads you know.

## Why your own roads feel impossible right now

Stand at Hazra crossing at 6 pm and count what is moving. Buses pulling in and out of the stop, yellow taxis cutting across, autos stopping wherever a passenger waves, people stepping off the pavement without looking. Ashutosh Mukherjee Road coming down from Bhowanipore meets it all at one signal.

Walk south along S.P. Mukherjee Road towards Kalighat and it doesn't get calmer. Near the temple side lanes, the crowd spills on to the road, flower sellers set up close to the kerb, and on busy temple days the lanes towards Kalighat Road barely move. Further on, Tollygunge Phari is a big junction where several roads meet and nobody waits their turn.

None of this is a place to find your balance. So we split it: quiet ground first, your own roads later.

## Scooty training for Kalighat learners: how the classes run

The [Basic Scooty Training](/courses/basic-scooty-training) course is 15 one-to-one classes of about 30 minutes. One trainer, one learner. You can use our scooty or bring your own.

**Classes 1 to 3** are about getting comfortable: sitting, holding the handle, finding both brakes, and feeling what a small twist of the throttle does. **Classes 4 to 6** are balance on a quiet stretch near our centre. **Classes 7 to 9** are turns. **Classes 10 to 12** move to quiet lanes, and **13 to 15** are about riding on your own.

One learner told us on camera, "Today is day 9 and I could lift my feet." Some do it on day 4. Nobody is rushed.

If you already ride a little but Hazra traffic freezes you, the [Advanced Scooty Training](/courses/advanced-scooty-training) course is 10 classes on busy roads, junctions, speed breakers and defensive riding. That's the one that prepares you for the Phari and the temple lanes.

## Three things this area will ask of you

**Slow riding without putting a foot down.** Near Kalighat, traffic often moves at walking pace. Riders who can't balance slowly keep dropping a foot, and that's when the scooty tips. We practise this a lot.

**Watching people, not just vehicles.** Around the temple and Hazra market, people step out from between parked cars. You learn to keep a finger on the brake and your eyes on the pavement edge.

**Smooth starts at a signal.** At Hazra crossing the light turns green and everyone moves at once. A jerky throttle here is how people lose confidence. Smooth, small twists fix it.
