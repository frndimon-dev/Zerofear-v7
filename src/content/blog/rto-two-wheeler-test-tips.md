The practical two-wheeler test checks whether you can control your vehicle calmly. Most failures come from small, fixable mistakes.

## What the test usually checks

- **Figure 8.** Riding an "8" shape slowly without putting your feet down.
- **Slow-speed balance.** Staying steady at walking pace.
- **Starting and stopping** smoothly where you're asked.
- **Indicators and observation** when turning.

## Common reasons people fail

1. **Putting a foot down** during the figure 8.
2. **Too much speed** in the turns of the 8.
3. **Forgetting indicators.**
4. **Sudden braking** that makes the vehicle wobble.

## How to practise the figure 8

Look ahead to the next curve, not at the front wheel. Keep the throttle steady and very gentle, and use the rear brake lightly to control speed. Practise both directions.
