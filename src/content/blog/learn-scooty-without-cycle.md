Many people believe you must know how to ride a cycle before learning a scooty. **That's not true.** At Zero Fear, most of our beginners have never ridden a cycle.

## Why a scooty is different

A cycle needs pedalling and balance at the same time. A scooty has **no pedals**: you sit, keep your feet ready on the ground, and use a gentle twist of the throttle. That makes the first steps much easier.

## Where we start

1. **Getting to know the scooty.** Posture, handle, throttle and both brakes. Your trainer moves the scooty with you.
2. **Feet-down balance.** You move at walking pace with your feet close to the ground, so you never feel you might fall.
3. **Smooth throttle.** Tiny twists, never sudden ones.
4. **Start and stop.** Controlled starts and stops, again and again, until they feel natural.

## Then what?

Once start, stop and balance feel easy, we add turns, then quiet roads, then normal traffic. You move ahead only when you're ready. That is our rule: **confidence before speed**.
