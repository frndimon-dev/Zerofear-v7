Want to learn **bike riding in Kolkata**? A motorcycle feels harder than a scooty because of the **clutch and gears**. With the right order of practice, it becomes simple. Zero Fear's bike training takes you there step by step.

## What you need before you start

You should be able to **balance a cycle**. On a bike, your hands and feet work the clutch, gear, throttle and brakes together, so basic balance should already feel natural.

## The 15-class plan

| Classes | What you learn |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Posture, handle, brakes, starting and stopping |
| 4–6 | Releasing the clutch, choosing gears, moving off smoothly |
| 7–9 | Clutch + gear + throttle together, changing gears while moving |
| 10–12 | Road riding: observation, traffic, turning, speed control |
| 13–15 | Fixing weak spots, more road time, RTO test practice |

## Honest note

Finishing the course means you can ride confidently in normal conditions. Every bike's weight and power is different, so take time to get used to a new or more powerful bike.
