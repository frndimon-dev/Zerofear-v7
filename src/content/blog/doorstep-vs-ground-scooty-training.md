Doorstep scooty training in Kolkata sounds like the obvious choice: why travel when the trainer can come to you? Sometimes it really is the better choice. Sometimes it costs more and teaches you less. Here is how we'd decide if we were in your place.

## Doorstep scooty training in Kolkata: where it wins

**When getting out is the hard part.** A mother with a small child at home. Someone looking after an elderly parent. A person whose evenings vanish in a commute. If the choice is "doorstep" or "never start", doorstep wins every time.

**When you'll ride the same roads later.** If you're learning so you can go to the market, the school gate or the station near your home, learning in those lanes means the last classes are spent on the exact roads you'll use. Areas we often go to include Garia, Jadavpur, Ranikuthi, Behala, Haridevpur, Naktala, Bansdroni, New Alipore, Bagha Jatin, Thakurpukur and Hazra.

**When you have your own scooty.** You learn on the vehicle you'll actually ride, with its own brakes, its own weight, its own height. Learners can bring their own scooty to ground classes too, but at home it's already there.

**When nerves are the biggest problem.** Some people simply relax more on familiar ground. A learner who isn't frightened learns faster.

## Ground classes: where they win

**Cost.** Classes near our centre start from ₹2,500. That gap is real money, and the training is the same.

**A quiet place for the first week.** Classes 4 to 6 are about balance, and balance needs room. Some home lanes are perfect. Others have a parked car every ten feet, a tea stall at the corner and a rickshaw that won't move. Near our centre, we already know the calm stretches and the times they're calm.

**No audience.** At home, the neighbours watch. The aunty on the balcony comments. Your cousin wants to "help". For many people, especially in the first few classes, learning where nobody knows them is easier. That surprises people, but it comes up a lot.

**Metro access.** If you live near the Blue Line, from Kalighat down to Kavi Subhash, getting to Netaji station is often quicker than you think. Many learners come straight from office and walk over.

## An honest check before choosing doorstep

Doorstep works best when a few things are true. Ask yourself:

1. Is there a lane, a para road or an open space within a few minutes of home where the first classes can happen without heavy traffic?
2. Is there a safe place to park and start the scooty?
3. Will you be home at the class time, ready, most days?

If the answer to the first one is a clear no, say so when you call. We'd rather tell you honestly that ground classes will suit you better than take a higher fee and have you wobble between parked cars.

## Still not sure?

Tell us about your lane, your timing and your scooty, if you have one, and we'll suggest what fits. We won't push the more expensive option.

## Who should pick which

**Pick doorstep** if leaving home is the real barrier, you have your own scooty, there's a quiet lane nearby, and the extra fee is comfortable for you.

**Pick ground classes** if you want to keep the cost down, your lane is busy and narrow, you'd rather learn away from familiar eyes, or you live close to the Blue Line.

Either way, the steps are the same: get to know the scooty, balance, turns, quiet roads, then riding on your own. One learner told us after two or three classes she was riding by herself. Another lifted her feet on day 9. Both are normal, wherever the class happens.
