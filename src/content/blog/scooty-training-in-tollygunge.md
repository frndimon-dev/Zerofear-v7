Looking for **scooty training in Tollygunge**? Zero Fear trains complete beginners in the lanes around **Netaji Metro and Kudghat**, so you can learn close to home, on calm roads, with a trainer beside you the whole time.

## Who our Tollygunge classes are for

- **Complete beginners.** You have never touched a scooty before.
- **People who can't ride a cycle.** A scooty does not need cycling skills. We start with feet-down balance.
- **Nervous riders.** You tried once, got scared, and stopped.
- **Women of any age.** Students, working women, homemakers and grandmothers all learn with us.

## How a typical first week looks

1. **Day 1–3:** Get to know the scooty. Posture, handle, throttle, both brakes. The trainer walks with you.
2. **Day 4–6:** Balance and slow, straight riding. Start, move, stop, again and again.
3. **Day 7 onwards:** Turns, quiet Tollygunge lanes, and then gentle traffic when you are ready.

The full [Basic Scooty Training](/courses/basic-scooty-training) course is 15 classes of about 30 minutes each.

## Why learn in Tollygunge?

Tollygunge has a good mix of **quiet residential lanes** for early practice and **real traffic roads** like Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Road for later classes. That means you learn balance in peace, then build confidence on the exact roads you will ride every day.
