You leave office, the metro is packed, and by the time you reach home the day is gone. That's the usual reason people put off learning to ride for another year. **Evening scooty classes in Kolkata** exist exactly for this, and at Zero Fear, scooty training after office is something we fix around your timing, one learner at a time.

## Who evening scooty classes suit

Mostly people whose days are not their own. Office staff in Salt Lake or Park Street who get back to the south by early evening. Teachers and bank employees. Nurses coming off a day shift. College students with afternoon labs. Parents who can only step out once someone else is home with the kids.

It also suits people who want to ride to work in the first place. If your plan is to take a scooty to office instead of the 6 pm bus, learning in the evening means you learn in the same light and traffic you'll ride in.

It suits you less if you're very tired after work and can't focus for 30 minutes. A sleepy learner learns slowly. If weekdays leave you drained, a mix of a couple of evenings plus a weekend morning works better. We'll talk it through.

## How to fit 15 classes around work

Our [Basic Scooty Training](/courses/basic-scooty-training) is 15 classes of about 30 minutes each. [Bike Training](/courses/bike-training) is also 15. Half an hour is short enough to squeeze in after office and long enough to make progress.

Here's how it usually fits:

| Classes per week | Roughly how long for 15 |
|---|---|
| 3 evenings | about 5 weeks |
| 4 evenings | about 4 weeks |
| 2 evenings + 1 weekend | about 5 weeks |

Regular beats occasional. Three classes in one week stick far better than three classes spread over three weeks, because your hands and feet remember what they did two days ago. Our post on [how many days it takes to learn scooty](/blog/how-many-days-to-learn-scooty) explains why.

A few things that help:

- **Pick fixed days.** Monday, Wednesday, Friday is easier to protect than "whenever I'm free".
- **Keep flat shoes in your bag.** Office heels and slippers are not for the first class.
- **Eat something small before.** Thirty minutes of concentration on an empty stomach after a long day is harder than it sounds.
- **Tell us early if a day breaks.** Office meetings run late. A message in time lets us move the slot.

Our centre is a short walk from **Netaji Metro (Kudghat)** on the Blue Line, so many learners get off the metro on the way home and walk over. If even that is too much, [doorstep training](/doorstep-training) brings the trainer to your area, within 10 km of Netaji Metro.

## What riding in the evening teaches you

Daytime learners sometimes get a shock the first time they ride after dark. Evening learners don't, because they've been doing it from the start. Here's what changes once the sun goes down.

**Lights, and how to use them.** You learn to check the headlight before you start, use the low beam in traffic, and switch to high beam only on an empty stretch. You learn to switch the indicator on early, because in the dark it's often the only thing the car behind sees.

**Glare.** An oncoming bus or car on high beam can blind you for a second. The fix is simple once someone shows you: look slightly to the left edge of the road, not into the lights, and slow down until your eyes adjust.

**Things you can't see well.** A speed breaker without paint. A pothole full of water that looks like flat road. A cycle with no light. An auto stopping suddenly to pick up a passenger. In the evening, you learn to ride a little slower and leave more gap, because you'll see these things later than you would at noon.

**Real 6 pm traffic.** Rush hour on N.S.C. Bose Road is not a quiet lane. In the early classes you won't be anywhere near it. But by the end of the course, once your trainer feels you're ready, an evening learner has already seen buses pulling out, rickshaws drifting across and people crossing between cars. That's the traffic you'll ride home in.

If you can already ride but evening traffic is what scares you, our [Advanced Scooty Training](/courses/advanced-scooty-training) is 10 classes on busy roads, junctions, speed breakers and defensive riding.

## Will I be on dark roads in my first class?

No. The order is the same as the day course. Classes 1 to 3 are getting to know the scooty, 4 to 6 are balance, 7 to 9 are turning, and only then quiet roads, then riding on your own. You learn balance where it's calm and well lit, and the roads come later.

One of our learners said it simply: "If you're still thinking about it, stop worrying. Just get in touch." That applies to evenings too.
