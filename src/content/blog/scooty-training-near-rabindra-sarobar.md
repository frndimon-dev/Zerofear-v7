If you live near **Rabindra Sarobar, Lake Gardens, Southern Avenue or Dhakuria**, you can learn to ride a scooty without travelling far. Zero Fear runs beginner-first, one-to-one classes across South Kolkata.

## Learn on calm roads first

The lanes around the lake area are wide and relatively calm in the mornings. That makes them good for the early part of training:

- **Balance practice** at walking pace
- **Smooth throttle control** without sudden jerks
- **Braking and stopping** exactly where you want

Once you are comfortable, your trainer moves you to normal traffic around Southern Avenue and Rashbehari.

## Can't ride a cycle?

That's fine. Most of our learners can't. A scooty is balanced differently, and we start with your feet on the ground. Read more in [Can you learn a scooty without knowing how to cycle?](/blog/learn-scooty-without-cycle)
