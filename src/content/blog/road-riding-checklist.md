Before you ride in Kolkata traffic, tick off these skills. If one feels shaky, practise it more on a quiet road first.

## The checklist

- **Slow-speed balance.** Ride at 5–10 km/h in a jam without putting your feet down.
- **Mirrors and indicators.** Check the mirror and switch on the indicator *before* every turn.
- **U-turn control.** Turn on a narrow road in one go, without putting your feet down.
- **Safe distance.** Keep at least one vehicle's gap from the vehicle in front.
- **Smooth braking.** Rear brake first, then front, without skidding.
- **Calm stops.** Stop exactly at a line without panic.
