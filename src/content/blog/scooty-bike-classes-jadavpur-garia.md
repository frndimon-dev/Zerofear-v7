If you've been searching for **scooty training in Jadavpur**, here's the honest answer first: we don't have a centre in Jadavpur. Our only centre is near Netaji Metro in Paschim Putiary, about 3 to 5 km from most of Jadavpur, Santoshpur, Baghajatin and Garia, and plenty of people from your side make that trip.

This page covers how to get to us, why your own roads are a hard place to start, and when it's better to have a trainer come to your lane instead.

## Why the roads near 8B are a tough first classroom

Stand at the 8B bus stand for five minutes around 9 in the morning. Buses pull in and out without much warning. Autos stop wherever someone waves. Students come out of the university gates in groups and cross wherever there's a gap. Raja S.C. Mullick Road is like that most of the day, and after 5 pm the moving part of the road shrinks to one lane.

The lanes behind the main road aren't calm either. In Santoshpur, cars are parked on both sides and you get a blind corner every hundred metres. Around Baghajatin, a quiet lane can suddenly open onto the main road with a bus right there. Down at Garia, the crowd near the bazar and the autos lined up near Kavi Nazrul metro leave very little room for someone who is still finding the brake.

Then there's E.M. Bypass. Many people from Santoshpur and the Bypass side eventually want to ride there, for office or a hospital visit, and it's fast. Cars change lanes without signalling and buses don't slow down for a wobbling scooty. It's a fine goal, but not where anyone should start.

This is why a cousin teaching you "just in the lane behind the house" so often ends with one scare near a parked car and the scooty sitting under a cover for months.

## What you learn when you come to us

Classes are one-to-one and about 30 minutes each. Our practice scooty is ready for you, or you can bring your own.

Complete beginners take [Basic Scooty Training](/courses/basic-scooty-training): 15 classes that go from sitting on the scooty, to balance, to turns, to quiet roads, to riding on your own. You don't need to know a cycle. If you can already ride but freeze at the Jadavpur crossing or on the Bypass, the [Advanced Scooty Training](/courses/advanced-scooty-training) is 10 classes on busy roads, junctions, speed breakers and defensive riding.

For geared motorcycles there's [Bike Training](/courses/bike-training), also 15 classes: getting to know the bike first, then clutch and gears, then putting clutch, gear and throttle together, then road riding.

A lot of people from this side are students or office-goers. Evening classes after office hours are possible. Tell us your free times when you call and we'll work around them.

## When doorstep training makes more sense

Sometimes the trip is the problem. Maybe you're looking after a parent at home, or you want to learn on your own scooty in the lanes you'll actually ride. All of Jadavpur, Santoshpur, Baghajatin and Garia sit within our 10 km doorstep area, and Jadavpur, Bagha Jatin and Garia are places we often cover.

The fee depends on how far you are from Netaji Metro / Kudghat:

Most homes in this area are more than 3 km away, so your fee will be in the third row. The calculator on the [doorstep training page](/doorstep-training) shows your exact amount. With doorstep classes, your trainer picks the quietest stretch near your home and a time when it's calm, so your first classes still happen away from the main road.

## Which one should you choose?

Come to the centre if you can manage the trip. The practice roads around Putiary and Kudghat are quieter than anything near 8B, and the course fee is lower.

Choose doorstep if travel would make you skip classes. Three classes in a week stick far better than three classes spread over three weeks, and a missed week usually means relearning the last lesson.

One learner put it simply in our feedback video: *"If you're still thinking about it, stop worrying. Just get in touch."*
