Every beginner makes mistakes. Knowing the common ones in advance makes learning faster and safer.

## 1. Too much throttle

When nervous, beginners often grip and twist the throttle hard. The scooty jumps forward and fear grows. **Fix:** twist only a little at a time, and keep your wrist relaxed.

## 2. Grabbing the front brake suddenly

A hard pull on the front brake can make the handle turn and the scooty skid. **Fix:** use the rear brake first to slow down, then add the front brake gently.

## 3. Speeding into turns

Accelerating into a turn is one of the most dangerous habits. **Fix:** release the throttle *before* the turn, slow down, then turn.

## 4. Looking down at the front wheel

Your scooty goes where your eyes go. **Fix:** look ahead to where you want to go, not at the ground.

## 5. Rushing to real traffic

Entering traffic too early creates panic. **Fix:** master start, stop, balance and turns in a quiet place first.
