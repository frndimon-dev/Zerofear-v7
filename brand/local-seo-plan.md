# Zero Fear — Google Maps / local ranking plan

Google ranks local results on three things: **relevance** (name, category, services, website text),
**distance** (how close the searcher is) and **prominence** (reviews, photos, links, activity).
Distance can't be changed, so Zero Fear wins Tollygunge / south Kolkata first and grows outward.
Nobody can guarantee #1, but this plan follows Google's rules and doesn't put the profile at risk.

## Once (setup)
| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Name, categories, description, services, service area, website as in `social-profiles.md`. Opening hours, “Women-owned”/“Appointment required” attributes if true. 10+ real photos (logo, cover, classes, trainers, practice ground). |
| Website | Done: same name/address/phone in the footer and in the Google data (schema `DrivingSchool`, service areas), sitemap in Search Console. |
| Instagram / Facebook | Same name, phone, address, website link (`social-profiles.md`). Website link in both. |
| Free listings (same name/address/phone, exactly) | Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, UrbanPro, Facebook. One listing each, no duplicates. |

## Every week
1. **Reviews:** ask every learner after the last class (WhatsApp review link). Don't pay, offer
   discounts for reviews, or write them yourself — Google deletes them and can suspend the profile.
   Reply to every review, naturally mentioning the course (“Glad the scooty classes in Behala went well”).
2. **Photos:** 3–4 new real class photos on Google (no faces of people who didn't agree).
3. **Google post:** one post (learner story, tip, offer) with a “Call” or “Book” button.
4. **Instagram / Facebook:** 2–3 posts or reels; first caption line names the skill and area.

## Every month
- One new area blog post on the website (Behala, Jadavpur, Garia, Naktala, …).
- Check Search Console (which searches show the site) and Google profile Performance.

## Never
- Keywords or place names in the business name.
- A second Google profile or a fake address.
- Changing name, address and phone at the same time.
