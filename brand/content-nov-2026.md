### 1 — New name
Best Two Wheeler Training School is now Zero Fear Scooty & Bike Training! 🛵

New name — same trainers, same patient one-to-one classes, same place near Netaji Metro, Tollygunge. Thank you to every learner who trusted us and left a review.

Learn to ride a scooty or bike even if you can't ride a cycle. Doorstep training is available within 10 km.

Fear Out. Freedom In.

### 2 — Beginners
Can't ride a cycle? You can still ride a scooty.

At Zero Fear, every class is one-to-one with a patient trainer. You start with balance, throttle and braking on a calm road, and move to real Kolkata traffic only when you feel ready. Our women-friendly trainers teach ladies, students and older learners every day.

Scooty and bike training near Netaji Metro, Tollygunge.

### 3 — RTO test + doorstep
Getting ready for your two-wheeler RTO driving test?

We practise the exact test movements — figure-8, slow balance and controlled turns — until they feel easy. Scooty and bike learners both welcome.

Can't come to us? We come to you. Doorstep training within 10 km of Netaji Metro: Behala, Jadavpur, Garia, Naktala, Bansdroni, Kudghat and New Alipore.

### 4 — Ladies
Scooty training for ladies, at your own pace.

Many of our learners are women who were nervous on day one. Our trainers stay beside you, explain every step calmly and never rush you. One-to-one classes near Netaji Metro, Tollygunge, or at your doorstep within 10 km.

Ride to work, to college or to the market — on your own.

### 5 — Bike training
Want to ride a bike (motorcycle)?

Clutch, gears and throttle can feel confusing at first. We teach them one step at a time: first on a quiet road, then turning, braking and riding in traffic, with a trainer beside you. Bike training for complete beginners and for scooty riders moving up.

### 6 — Any age
It's never too late to learn to ride.

Students, working professionals, mothers and grandparents — our learners start at every age. With one-to-one classes and a patient trainer, you go at the speed that feels safe for you.

Scooty training near Netaji Metro, Tollygunge, and doorstep classes within 10 km.

### 7 — Safety tip
Safe riding starts on day one.

Three habits we teach every learner:
• Helmet on and strapped before you start.
• Use both brakes together, gently.
• Look far ahead, not at the front wheel.

Learn these and more in our one-to-one scooty and bike classes.

### 8 — First solo ride
From "I'm scared" to riding alone.

On day one our trainer walks beside you and holds the scooty. A few classes later, you ride on your own — that's the moment we work for. Join the learners who now ride to work, to college and to the market every day.

Fear Out. Freedom In.
